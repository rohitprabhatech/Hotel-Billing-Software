## Vision

One multi-tenant SaaS for **14** business types: Common Core + Industry Modules.  
Monthly subscription with configurable free trial (default 15 days).

## Architecture principle

Do **not** build 14 separate apps. Extend shared billing, inventory, customers, payments, reports; enable industry packs by business type.

## Supported businesses

See [supported-business-types.md](./supported-business-types.md).  
**Excluded:** Medical Stores (and medicine/prescription entities).

Existing product already has Master Admin, registration approval, plans/trial, bills, stock movements, WhatsApp/email, AI, audit. V2 adds industry configuration and missing CRM/procurement/industry packs.
